# 100 Phrases Across 12 Verb Tenses

Vocabulary used from `specifications.md`:
- **Verbs:** give, tell, work, call, try, ask
- **Nouns:** bee, ant, spider, fly, butterfly, fox, wolf
- **Pronouns:** he, she, it, they
- **Adjectives:** tall, good, beautiful
- **Adverbs:** now, yesterday, soon, already, enough, consequently

---

## 1. Simple Present

1. He gives the bee a good leaf now.
2. She tells the ant a beautiful story.
3. It works enough every day.
4. They call the fox soon.
5. He asks the wolf a good question.
6. She tries hard enough now.
7. They give the spider a tall home.
8. It tells the fly the truth now.
9. He works consequently every morning.
10. She calls the butterfly a beautiful name.
