# English Verbs Table with Spanish Translations

A consolidated reference table of English verbs with their Spanish translations and four principal parts (base, past, past participle, present participle), plus the regular/irregular type. Derived from `verbs.md`.

The **Tier** column groups verbs by frequency:

- **Core Verbs** – the ~100 most common verbs (originally Tier 1)
- **Basic** – everyday and common verbs for fluency (consolidation of original Tiers 2 and 3)
- **Intermediate** – standard but less frequent verbs (originally Tier 4)
- **Advanced & Lower-frequency** – formal, literary, technical, or specialized verbs (originally Tier 5)

The **Spanish** column gives the Spanish translation of the Base verb (infinitive form).
